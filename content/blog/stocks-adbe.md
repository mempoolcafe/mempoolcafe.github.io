## Bottom Line
There are real fears to Adobe's growth due to AI, but the extend of these fears are unjust. The fears are reasonable about growth and pricing power but are overstated about extinction. 

1. Business is still healthy. Record earnings, 97% subscription revenue, 10.6B trailing free cashflow.
2. AI is pressuring organic growth to around 8% and free / lower tier subscriptions are facing headwinds in light of low cost competitors. 
3. Threat is mostly from general purpose AI such as ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and cheap alternatives from CapCut, Canva, and Figma that absorb recreational and casual work. However, Pro and enterprise subscriptions remain consistent and sticky. 
4. Adobe has been using rival models and agents with enterprise workflows, but AI ARR has only been around 2.4% of total ARR.
5. Market currently is pricing in 0 Growth. At 14x trailing earnings and 11% free cashflow with heavy buybacks, the case for Adobe becomes whether it becomes a slower-growth cash machine or it reaccelerates. 

## Current Valuation
Adobe is still pushing out double digit growth while posting a record profits, however the underlying subscription growth is slowying and AI is raising its costs. 

Revenue is up 13%, Total ARR up 11.2%,  AI-first ARR over 150%, Non-GAAP operating margin (profibility excluding 1 time events) down 46.3%, free cash flow 40.8% of revenue, Revenue target about +12%.

## Market Pricing

Currently the market is pricing as if Adobe has stopped growing, despite record revenue and earnings. From FY21 to FY26, price to earnings fell 80% while diluted GAAP EPS is up 79%. Share prices fell ebcause investors pay for far less for each dollar of profit and not because profit fell. 

At around a $92B market cap, trailing cash flow of $10.6B is around a 11.4% yield and with a 9.5% cost of equity the implied long-run cash flow equares to a growth of -1% to +1% a year. 

## Competitive Landscape
There isn't one main Adobe killer, but rather the threats are multiple general purpose AI platforms that compete for everyday creative tasks. ChatGPT Images, Gemini's Nano Banana, Canva, Figma, Claude Design, Apple Creator Studio, Capcut, Midjourney, and all other AI video/image studios are Adobe competitors for  design/image generation.

How has Adobe responded? Adobe AI ARR has grown more than 150% but is only 2.4% of total ARR, but management says that the free tier will pay off in 2027. 
Adobe Actions
1. Adobe has aggregated models and trained models based on own licensed content (image, video, audio, vector, and 3D).
2. Integrated agents inside apps. Creative Agent that works across Photoshop, Premiere, Illustrator, InDesign, etc. 
3. Shows up inside everychatbut. Allows users to connect to ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini
4. Free Subscriptions to Firefly, Express, and Acrobat experiences. 
5. Owning the content supply chain and aquiring Semrush (AI Search Layer). 


## Scenarios
Bull: 25% | ARR Growth Back to 11-13%, Margins ~45%, free users convert, enterprise pay for agents. 
Base: 50% | Pro and enterprise sales stay the same, Acrobat and enterprise AI offset consumer losses. 
Bear: 20% | Chatbot and agents become the default creative interface and AI-native tools reach Pro quality.
Severe: 5% | Pro workflows replaced by agents and open source tools, Acrobat commoditized. 

## Buybacks!
Adobe is literally returning almost all their free cashflow through buybacks and at current pricing it reduces the share count quickly. Buybacks have used 92% of freecashflow since Fiscal Year 2021.

At $243 a share, free cashflow equals around 11.2% of Adobes entire market value, while employee stock grants takes around 2%. That means that if all free cash flow were to be spent it woul dbe around 9% of shares a year if the price stayed flat. With a estimated 9% growth in free cash flow, around 16% of net shares would be retired at current pace. As long as the market stops marking down Adobes multiple, buybacks can easily lift the stocks around 6-10% a year without business growth.

## Conclusion
Adobe at $243 a cautious buy (reasonable entry). A hard value buy around $190-210 (very strong margin of safety). The business is healthy with about $26.6B in revenue and $10.6B in free cash flow; 41% cash margins. AI tools like Figma, Canva, and ChatGPT are slowing growth and weaking pricing power, while designers are unhappy with AI narrative. However, the moat is its Pro and Enterprise subscriptions where there isn't much of a real substitute(forced to use the product). Market currently prices in no growth at 9x free cash flow. Heavy buybacks also add a cusion by increasing EPS. My DCF analysis puts the fair value around $377 with a bear case around $210. My current thesis breaks if the earning results on Dec 9 show that organic growth is heading to sub 5%. I will be opening a modest position in ADBE. This is not finanical advice.
